Why Choose Us?

You can additional subtitle Here.

  • Unique Architecture

    Designed to blend seamlessly with its natural surroundings, the property features distinctive architecture that combines timeless elegance with contemporary comfort.

  • Location

    Situated about 45 minutes from the northern entrance of the Sinharaja Rainforest, a UNESCO World Heritage site, the resort is ideal for nature lovers and wildlife enthusiasts.

  • Staff and service

    Past visitors often praise the staff for their attentiveness and excellent hospitality.

  • Amenities

    Facilities include a spring-water swimming pool, a cave bar, and an open-air restaurant serving Sri Lankan and Western cuisine.

  • Activities

    The resort organizes guided nature walks, hikes, and excursions to the Sinharaja Rainforest. Other potential activities include kayaking and mountain biking.

  • Wildlife

    Our grounds are alive with nature. Guests can often spot playful monkeys, curious squirrels, and an array of endemic bird species that make the estate their home.
